How it works

This is a procedural house music generator that creates an endless, evolving track. The arrangement automatically cycles through 16 sections, bringing elements in and out to create tension and release.

Features:

  • Four-on-the-floor kick at 124 BPM
  • Offbeat hi-hats with occasional open hats
  • Claps on beats 2 and 4
  • Syncopated bassline following the chord progression
  • Rhodes-style 7th chord stabs
  • Warm sustaining pads
  • Congas and percussion for groove
  • Reverb, delay, and filter sweep effects

Console API: HouseMusic.start(), HouseMusic.stop(), HouseMusic.toggleElement('kick')
